Entry for make.fun's bounty **"Sum up 24 November's world news in one image"**
(slug `news-image-20261124`).

The entry image is a one-page newspaper poster that sums up a single day —
**Tuesday, 24 November 2026**, named in large type under the masthead. It
carries one lead story, five further stories across regions and fields, and
an "Also that day" strip:

- **Lead:** a Houthi strike hit a tower in Riyadh's financial district for
  the second time in a week, injuring 11.
- A Russian strike on Zaporizhzhia killed two and wounded 40.
- Israeli strikes in Gaza killed Hamas's internal security chief and six
  others, despite the ceasefire.
- Landmine casualties reached a five-year high (Landmine Monitor 2026).
- US consumer confidence jumped to 103.3, its highest since January 2025.
- The WHO reported three US bird flu (H5N1) cases, one of them fatal.
- Also that day: Ebola teams trapped in a Congo hospital; Israel's Eisenkot
  signed a first coalition pact; ousted PM Hasina said she will return to
  Bangladesh on 16 December.

Every story happened, or was first reported, on 24 November 2026 and was
confirmed from at least two independent outlets; see **SOURCES.md** for the
story-by-story sourcing.

## How the image was made

The poster is drawn in code, not generated by an image model, so every word
is spelled exactly as written. `poster/poster.html` is the page template,
`poster/news-data.json` holds the stories as data, and the flat illustrations
are hand-drawn inline SVG. `poster/render.mjs` fills the template from the
data and screenshots it in headless Chromium at exactly 1024×1024, producing
`assets/entry.png` (PNG, 1024×1024, ~187 KB).

## Running the entry

```
python3 main.py                      # writes /output/entry.png
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py   # or a directory of your choice
```

`main.py` uses only the Python standard library, needs no network, and
copies `assets/entry.png` byte-for-byte, so repeated runs always produce an
identical file.
